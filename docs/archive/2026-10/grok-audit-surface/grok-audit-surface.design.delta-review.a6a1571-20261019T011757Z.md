## Summary
This is an advisory delta review of design v1.4 (`a6a1571a`) against the 3 must-fixes and 4 should-fixes of the v1.3 delta review. All seven have an answering hunk, and I checked the ones that make claims about the tree. On the real `measure_effort`, the renderer-pin regex yields exactly the seven shapes. A drifted log of `response.*` types gets shape `unparseable`, or `parsed` after one `{"event":"init"}` line. Both render an item that matches the non-grok counts regex. `missing`, `empty` and `codex-text` render the wrong-file forms. A 65-deep nesting gives `unparseable` and a 64-deep one gives `grok`. Each marked count-wire line appears once in the fenced blocks. The reset guards' fall-through tokens are distinct, so the P-rows kill. The step-3 fix and the count-wire fix close their findings, and the five-guard precondition closes the empty, mistyped or foreign target. One fixture defect blocks: two reset fixtures plant an extra entry in the live root to exercise step 6, but the re-walk added in v1.3 sees that entry first, so they get `internal:RootResetMismatch`. Their asserted `root_git`/`leak` tokens never print. The remaining items are fixture gaps, an undefined type-set reading, symlink-following inside the reset, and two self-count slips.
Evidence: 11 files opened, 22 greps run.

## Must-fix
- `test_build_root_reset_runs_the_reach_check` (rewritten in v1.4) and `test_build_root_reset_rescans_after_removal` (unchanged from v1.3) cannot produce the tokens they assert. The cause is D5's own order of operations. After the removal and restore, the reset "re-walks the live root and asserts that its set of root-relative paths, and each file's sha256, equal the expected tree's", and on a difference it raises `RootResetMismatch`. Only "Then" does step 6 (scan, then reach) run on the live root. The reach fixture replaces the removal step with "one that plants a `.git` directory in the live root and removes nothing". The rescans fixture makes removal a no-op and plants a by-product. In both, the live root then holds a path the expected tree lacks, so the re-walk fires first and the token is `ROOT: UNREADABLE reason=internal:RootResetMismatch`, not `root_git:<path>` or `leak:<path>`. The re-walk can be patched on its own (`test_build_root_reset_mismatch_is_internal` patches it), so the "removal step" does not include it. The sentence "only the step-6 reach check can see it" is therefore false, because the re-walk sees it too. Possible repairs: (a) plant into the expected tree, by patching step 5 or `placements` so the extra entry is part of the build and the re-walk agrees, then let step 6 find it on the live root; (b) also patch the re-walk to pass, so the two fixtures test step 6 with the re-walk disarmed (state that this is what they test); or (c) plant in the window after the re-walk, with the re-walk patched to plant and then return equal. Option (c) is the most faithful reading of "a writer still running". Whichever is chosen, each fixture should assert that the re-walk passed (no `RootResetMismatch` on stdout), so the token it asserts is shown to come from step 6. I did not check whether any existing P-row targets these two tests. If one does, its kill is currently on the wrong guard.

- The "grok log's distinct `type` set" that `vocab_missing` reads is never defined: no line split, no depth bound. D11 still calls the `unparseable` case "guaranteed" and then states residual (2), which contradicts it. The two readings differ by construction. `scan_grok` splits on `"\n"`, needs a dict, skips `_json_deeper_than(event, 64)`, and catches `RecursionError` (`h-mad/scripts/h_mad_review_evidence.py:333-376`). The step-2 reading is unspecified. Residual (2) lists two members (depth, `RecursionError`) of an open class: lines that the two readings accept differently. Line splitting (`split("\n")` versus `splitlines()` on `\r` or ` `) is a third member. The `RecursionError` member is vacuous if both use `json.loads`, since the same input raises in both. Rule: define the step-2 type reading as `scan_grok`'s per-line acceptance (split on `"\n"`, `json.loads`, dict, not deeper than `GROK_MAX_DEPTH`, string `type`). The `unparseable` guarantee then holds, and residual (2) and its fixture go away. A log whose only typed lines are too deep then has no accepted line and is `input_unparseable` at step 2. Alternatively, keep the residual, but define the reading's split and depth exactly and drop "guaranteed".

- The reset body follows symlinks inside the live root. This predates v1.4 but belongs to the class v1.4's precondition opened. The precondition limits the target, not the entries the reset removes or rewrites. The reset removes absent-from-expected entries "whatever its kind", and rewrites "every expected file whose bytes differ". A cycle can leave a placed document replaced by a symlink to a path outside the root. The byte comparison reads through the link, the bytes differ, and the expected bytes are written through it to the outside path. Step 6's `leak:` on the symlink fires only afterwards. A symlinked directory among the by-products, removed by a walker that follows it, deletes outside the root. Repair: before comparing or writing, `os.lstat` every live entry. Remove a symlink, or an entry whose kind differs from the expected one, with `os.unlink` (never traversed). Then write expected files fresh, for example with `O_CREAT|O_EXCL|O_NOFOLLOW` after the unlink. Add one fixture each (placed file replaced by a symlink to a sentinel outside the root, and a symlinked by-product directory): the sentinel is unchanged and the reset reaches `ROOT: READY`. In that corner a PASS does not certify AC-1.1 ("reports `format=grok`"). Say so beside the residual, or raise an OPEN-DECISION for a spec reason.

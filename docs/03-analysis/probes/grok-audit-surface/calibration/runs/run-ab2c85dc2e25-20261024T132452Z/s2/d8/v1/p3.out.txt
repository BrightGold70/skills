## Summary

Design v1.4 covers the spec's acceptance criteria except two restatements: AC-5.2's UNWIRED verdict is told to exit 2, and AC-7.2 treats a malformed `6a-prime` entry as unrecorded. Three further spec requirements are not what an implementer would build: the caller never halts `step5:implementer_unresolved`, class (d) under an `unknown` scan denies `judge-error`, and three class-(b) tools keep no path keys after FR-8 capture.

## Summary
`docs/02-design/features/agy-coder-fallback.design.md` (v1.4) settles DO-1 through DO-7 thoroughly across FR-1..FR-10, but contains two internal/cross-document build contradictions in FR-5 and FR-2 that would misdirect an implementer. Specifically, §"FR-5" instructs the assembler's `UNWIRED` branch to `exit 2` (violating `Audit-gate signal discipline`, spec FR-5/AC-5.2, and the design's own docstring and mutation rules two paragraphs below), and §"FR-2" "The caller's halts (v1.4)" enumerates only two halt outcomes (`step5:implementer_switch_unrecorded` and `step5:implementer_invalid`), omitting `step5:implementer_unresolved` for `no-record` and `unreadable` tokens despite claiming in v1.4's version history to have adopted all three. Two non-blocking cross-document synchronization items between `design.md`, `spec.md` (v1.10), and `plan.md` (v1.6) are recorded under Should-fix.
